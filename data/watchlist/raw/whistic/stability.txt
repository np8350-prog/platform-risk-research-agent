Whistic was founded in **2015** and has been operating for approximately **11 years** (as of 2026). The company is headquartered in Pleasant Grove, Utah.**Funding:** Whistic has raised a total of **$51.1M** across 5 funding rounds. Key rounds include:- **Seed Round:** $115K on March 1, 2016- **Series A:** $12M on July 20, 2020, led by Emergence Capital- **Series B:** $35M on June 7, 2022, led by JMI Equity, with participation from Forgepoint Capital and existing investors Emergence Capital, Album VC, and FJ Labs**Team Size:** Employee estimates vary. PitchBook reports **76 employees** as of 2024. Other sources estimate 48 employees (as of June 2026), 80 employees (as of May 2024), or 51-200.**Leadership Departures:** Nick Sorensen served as CEO from 2017 until approximately August 2025. Juan Rodriguez is now listed as CEO and Founder as of August 2025.**Layoffs & Acquisition Rumors:** No known layoffs have been publicly reported. There is no evidence of Whistic being acquired. However, one third-party analysis notes that with ~$51M total raised and no later round since 2022, the company has a "smaller war chest than category neighbors, so acquisition risk". A Glassdoor review from October 2024 expressed concern that the company is "struggling and will layoff at any time," but this is an isolated, anonymous opinion, not confirmed by any public reporting.**Financial Distress:** No public signs of financial distress have been reported.
