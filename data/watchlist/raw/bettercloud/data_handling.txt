BetterCloud collects and stores identity attributes, SaaS application user logs, directory structures, administrative access logs, file-sharing metadata, and security policy event telemetry (https://www.bettercloud.com/privacy-statement/) (https://trust.bettercloud.com/). Hosting infrastructure relies primarily on public cloud providers including Google Cloud Platform (GCP) (https://trust.bettercloud.com/) and Microsoft Azure (https://www.gcpweekly.com/gcp-resources/tag/bigquery/). Data retention terms are dictated by customer enterprise contracts and Data Processing Agreements (DPAs), with individual privacy deletion requests processed within statutory 45-day timelines (https://www.bettercloud.com/privacy-statement/) (https://www.bettercloud.com/privacy-policy/). BetterCloud does not use customer data to train public or proprietary generative AI foundation models (no URL available for this). Subprocessors are utilized for infrastructure, messaging, and operational analytics, with Google LLC explicitly listed as an infrastructure hosting and data processing subprocessor (https://trust.bettercloud.com/).
