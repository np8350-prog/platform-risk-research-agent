**Data Collected & Stored:** BetterCloud collects personal information including first and last name, address, email, telephone number, department, job title, manager, profile picture, IP address, and other contact/job-related information provided when customers connect applications to the Services. Data also includes usage data from SaaS applications connected to the platform (email address, IP address, operating system, domain information).**Data Hosting:** BetterCloud hosts data in a multi-tenant, outsourced infrastructure provider (cloud). For European-based organizations, data at rest is placed in a **data center within the EU** to comply with data residency requirements. The platform is hosted on infrastructure providers audited for SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 compliance.**Data Retention Policy:** Not explicitly detailed in public documentation. The Product Privacy Statement does not specify retention periods.**AI Model Training:** No public information on whether BetterCloud uses customer data to train AI models. Not addressed in the Product Privacy Statement or Privacy Policy.**Subprocessors & Third Parties:** BetterCloud maintains a third-party security management program to ensure subprocessors meet security and privacy standards. Every subprocessor must have a data processing agreement including Standard Contractual Clauses or other valid data transfer mechanisms in compliance with GDPR. Specific subprocessor names are **not listed** in public documentation.**Vagueness:** The Product Privacy Statement notes that in certain instances, BetterCloud "may act as an independent controller or business" — creating some ambiguity about when BetterCloud is a processor vs. controller. The statement also clarifies that it does not apply to personal information collected on websites for marketing purposes.
