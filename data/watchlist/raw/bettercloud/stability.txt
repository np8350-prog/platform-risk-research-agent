BetterCloud was founded in 2011 by David Politis and formally incorporated on May 4, 2012 (https://tracxn.com/d/companies/bettercloud/__yJzr7NgOee3NbjvPemP4jncrl2e1y8RhRMset2J3a0w). Prior to its acquisition, the company raised approximately $186.9 million across 10 funding rounds (https://www.clay.com/dossier/bettercloud-funding). Key funding rounds include a $2.2 million Seed round in May 2012 (https://www.clay.com/dossier/bettercloud-funding), a $5.05 million Series A in January 2013 led by Flybridge (https://www.clay.com/dossier/bettercloud-funding), a $6 million Series B in September 2013 (https://www.clay.com/dossier/bettercloud-funding), an $8.6 million Series C in May 2014 (https://www.clay.com/dossier/bettercloud-funding), a $25 million Series D in March 2015 led by Accel (https://www.clay.com/dossier/bettercloud-funding), a $60 million Series E in April 2018 led by Bain Capital Ventures (https://www.clay.com/dossier/bettercloud-funding), a $5 million Corporate round in September 2019 from Dropbox (https://www.clay.com/dossier/bettercloud-funding), a $75 million Series F in May 2020 led by Warburg Pincus (https://www.clay.com/dossier/bettercloud-funding), and secondary market liquidity rounds in April 2021 and March 2022 (https://www.clay.com/dossier/bettercloud-funding). BetterCloud's estimated team size is between 101 and 250 employees (https://www.clay.com/dossier/bettercloud-funding), with historic peaks around 277 employees (https://tracxn.com/d/companies/bettercloud/__yJzr7NgOee3NbjvPemP4jncrl2e1y8RhRMset2J3a0w). The company conducted staff layoffs in May 2020 as part of broader macroeconomic adjustments across tech vendors (https://layoffs.fyi/companies/). Leadership changes include founder David Politis stepping down from CEO to Executive Chairman (https://tracxn.com/d/companies/bettercloud/__yJzr7NgOee3NbjvPemP4jncrl2e1y8RhRMset2J3a0w), followed by Jesse Levin serving as CEO (https://tracxn.com/d/companies/bettercloud/__yJzr7NgOee3NbjvPemP4jncrl2e1y8RhRMset2J3a0w), and Raj Kunnath being appointed President of BetterCloud in April 2026 (https://www.newswire.com/news/corestack-appoints-raj-kunnath-as-president-of-bettercloud-to-accelerate). No public shutdown rumors exist (no URL available for this). In terms of ownership transitions, private equity firm Vista Equity Partners acquired BetterCloud in mid-2022 (https://www.clay.com/dossier/bettercloud-funding), and cloud governance platform CoreStack acquired BetterCloud in March 2026 (https://zylo.com/blog/best-application-portfolio-management-software).
