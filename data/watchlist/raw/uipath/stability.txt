UiPath was founded in 2005 as DeskOver in Bucharest, Romania, by Daniel Dines and Marius Tirca, before rebranding to UiPath in 2015 (https://businessmodelcanvastemplate.com/blogs/brief-history/uipath-brief-history). Its primary venture funding rounds prior to going public included a $1.6M Seed round in 2015 (https://businessmodelcanvastemplate.com/blogs/brief-history/uipath-brief-history), a $30M Series A in April 2017 (https://forgeglobal.com/uipath_ipo/), a $153M Series B in March 2018 (https://forgeglobal.com/uipath_ipo/), a $225M Series C in November 2018 (https://forgeglobal.com/uipath_ipo/), a $568M Series D in April 2019 (https://forgeglobal.com/uipath_ipo/), a $225M Series E in July 2020 at a $10.2B valuation (https://forgeglobal.com/uipath_ipo/), and a $750M Series F in February 2021 at a $34.86B valuation (https://forgeglobal.com/uipath_ipo/). The company completed its IPO on the NYSE (ticker: PATH) on April 21, 2021, raising $1.34B at a valuation of $29.07B (https://forgeglobal.com/uipath_ipo/). UiPath maintains a global team size of approximately 4,000+ employees ("no URL available for this").

Regarding workforce reductions, UiPath laid off 5% of its workforce in early 2022 and 6% in late 2022 (https://siliconangle.com/2024/07/09/uipath-lays-off-10-workforce-push-increased-efficiency/). In July 2024, UiPath announced a 10% companywide workforce reduction (~420 employees) as part of an operational restructuring (https://siliconangle.com/2024/07/09/uipath-lays-off-10-workforce-push-increased-efficiency/). Leadership departures include CEO Rob Enslin abruptly stepping down in May 2024 (effective June 1, 2024) after two years in the role, causing shares to fall by nearly 30% alongside lowered revenue guidance (https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news-updates/uipath-stock-plunges-nearly-30-as-ceo-rob-enslin-abruptly-resigns). Co-founder Daniel Dines returned as sole CEO starting June 1, 2024 (https://www.romaniajournal.ro/business/uipath-ceo-rob-enslin-steps-down-romanian-billionaire-daniel-dines-returns-shares-drop/). There are no shutdown rumors or signs of critical financial distress, and UiPath remains a publicly traded vendor with over $1.5B in annual recurring revenue (ARR) (https://businessmodelcanvastemplate.com/blogs/brief-history/uipath-brief-history).
