UiPath cloud products collect user account data (names, emails, job titles), system performance logs, error logs, network logs, and transactional data generated during automation executions in Orchestrator or Data Service (https://www.uipath.com/legal/trust-and-security/privacy-policy). Multi-tenant SaaS environments are hosted primarily on Microsoft Azure and Amazon Web Services (AWS) data centers located across major global regions (US, EU, APAC) (https://docs.uipath.com/automation-cloud/automation-cloud/latest/admin-guide/customer-managed-keys). For customers with strict residency rules, on-premises deployment via UiPath Automation Suite ensures data remains within private infrastructure (https://www.uipath.com/legal/trust-and-security/privacy).

System execution logs are retained according to tenant configurations (typically 30 to 90 days) ("no URL available for this"), while personal data associated with platform accounts is deleted within 18 months after membership or usage ends (https://www.uipath.com/legal/trust-and-security/privacy-policy). UiPath explicitly does not use customer production data or prompts to train foundation AI models without customer consent, and offers controls to opt out of product improvement telemetry (https://www.uipath.com/legal/trust-and-security/privacy). Third-party subprocessors used for service delivery and operation include AWS, Microsoft Azure, Salesforce, Marketo, Gainsight, Cloudflare, and Tribble Inc. (https://www.uipath.com/legal/trust-and-security/privacy-policy).
