Recurring frustration patterns
Across platforms, several themes appear repeatedly:
	•	pricing increases as organizations scale
	•	uncertainty around enterprise licensing
	•	applications becoming harder to maintain as they grow
	•	debugging complex state interactions
	•	desire for more reusable architecture patterns
	•	proprietary nature of the platform
	•	occasional cloud reliability issues
These patterns appear consistently across multiple community sources rather than being isolated complaints.

Are users leaving for competitors?
Yes—but mostly for architectural or cost reasons.
The most commonly mentioned alternatives are:
	•	Appsmith
	•	Budibase
	•	ToolJet
	•	Microsoft Power Apps
	•	Internal custom React applications
Reasons users cite for considering a switch include:
	•	lower cost
	•	open-source options
	•	avoiding vendor lock-in
	•	greater customization
	•	self-hosting requirements
However, many discussions conclude that while these alternatives reduce licensing costs or increase flexibility, they often require more engineering effort than Retool.
The general sentiment is:
"Retool is expensive, but it saves enough developer time that many organizations still choose it."
This appears frequently in Reddit and Hacker News discussions.
