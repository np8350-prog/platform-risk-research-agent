OneTrust was founded in 2016 by Kabir Barday in Atlanta, Georgia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OneTrust). The company has raised over $1.13 billion in total venture funding across multiple investment rounds (https://tracxn.com/d/companies/onetrust/__ze9yaIbvxYzoxTM_yqDNZtaDbQ_ItyTV7ebRehscTWI): a $200 million Series A in July 2019 led by Insight Partners at a $1.3 billion valuation (https://www.clay.com/dossier/onetrust-funding); a $210 million Series B in February 2020 led by Coatue at a $2.7 billion valuation (https://www.clay.com/dossier/onetrust-funding); a $300 million Series C in December 2020 led by TCV at a $5.1 billion valuation (https://www.clay.com/dossier/onetrust-funding); a $210 million Series C extension in April 2021 (https://tracxn.com/d/companies/onetrust/__ze9yaIbvxYzoxTM_yqDNZtaDbQ_ItyTV7ebRehscTWI); and a $150 million Series D in July 2023 led by Generation Investment Management and Sands Capital at a $4.5 billion valuation (https://tracxn.com/d/companies/onetrust/__ze9yaIbvxYzoxTM_yqDNZtaDbQ_ItyTV7ebRehscTWI).

As of 2026, OneTrust maintains a global workforce of approximately 2,487 employees (https://tracxn.com/d/companies/onetrust/__ze9yaIbvxYzoxTM_yqDNZtaDbQ_ItyTV7ebRehscTWI). In June 2022, the company laid off roughly 950 employees (25% of its workforce) to pivot toward profitability amidst changing market conditions (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OneTrust). Founder Kabir Barday continues to lead the firm as CEO (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OneTrust), with no critical public leadership departures reported ("no URL available for this"). There are no active shutdown rumors or indications of financial distress ("no URL available for this"). Instead of being an acquisition target, OneTrust acts as an aggressive consolidator, acquiring companies such as DataGuidance, Integris Software, Docuvision, Tugboat Logic, Convercent, and Planetly (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OneTrust).
