Does Glean use customer data to train AI models?
Glean states that customer data is used to provide enterprise AI functionality and integrates with models including OpenAI/Azure OpenAI. (Glean("https://www.glean.com/website-terms?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
Glean's terms state that when using Azure OpenAI/OpenAI through Glean's service:
	•	customer data is transmitted only when generative AI features are explicitly used
	•	negotiated zero-day retention applies for those services. (Glean("https://www.glean.com/website-terms?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
I found no evidence that Glean uses customer enterprise data to train general-purpose models.
Assessment:
	•	Customer data used for retrieval/answer generation: Yes.
	•	Customer data used for general model training: No evidence found.
