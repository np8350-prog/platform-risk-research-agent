General sentiment across Reddit, G2, review sites and industry discussions:
Common praise
	•	Strong automation for continuous compliance evidence collection.
	•	Significantly reduces manual SOC 2 audit work.
	•	Large integration ecosystem.
	•	Modern user interface compared with older governance/compliance tools.
	•	Frequently viewed as a leading competitor to Vanta. (Reddit("https://www.reddit.com/r/BlackberryAI/comments/1ryu65o/drata/?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
Common complaints
	•	Premium pricing, especially for startups.
	•	Some customers mention implementation can be time-consuming depending on environment complexity.
	•	Certain integrations require additional configuration or maintenance.
	•	Feature expansion sometimes increases product complexity.
Recurring frustrations
	•	Additional compliance frameworks and advanced capabilities may require higher pricing tiers.
	•	Audit preparation still requires human involvement despite automation.
Users leaving for competitors
	•	Some discussions compare Drata with Vanta, Secureframe and Thoropass primarily based on pricing or preferred workflow.
	•	No widespread pattern suggesting customers are abandoning Drata because of reliability issues.
	•	Recent Reddit discussion following another vendor's controversy actually showed positive sentiment toward Drata rather than migration away from it. (Reddit("https://www.reddit.com/r/BlackberryAI/comments/1ryu65o/drata/?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
