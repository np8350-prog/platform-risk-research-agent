SOC 2
Status: SOC 2 Type II
OpenAI states that the infrastructure supporting the API and business ChatGPT offerings has undergone an independent SOC 2 Type II examination covering:
	•	Security
	•	Availability
	•	Confidentiality
	•	Privacy
A SOC 2 Type II report is performed by an independent CPA firm and evaluates the effectiveness of controls over a period of time, making it stronger evidence than a Type I report. OpenAI does not publicly identify the auditing firm on its main security page, but the examination itself is described as independent. (OpenAI("https://openai.com/security-and-privacy/?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
Evidence classification:
	•	✅ Independent third-party audit (reported by the vendor)

GDPR
OpenAI does not claim to be "GDPR certified" (there is no general GDPR certification).
Instead, OpenAI states that it:
	•	supports customers' compliance with GDPR
	•	offers a Data Processing Addendum (DPA)
	•	provides privacy controls
	•	offers enterprise data-handling features intended to help organizations meet GDPR obligations
This is an important distinction:
	•	OpenAI claims its services support GDPR compliance
	•	It does not claim an independent certification that "OpenAI is GDPR compliant," because GDPR is a legal framework rather than a certification standard. (OpenAI("https://openai.com/security-and-privacy/?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
Evidence classification:
	•	⚠️ Vendor claim

ISO 27001
Status: ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified
OpenAI states it maintains ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification for the information security management systems supporting:
	•	OpenAI API
	•	ChatGPT Enterprise
	•	ChatGPT Edu
It also lists additional ISO certifications:
	•	ISO 27017 (cloud security)
	•	ISO 27018 (protection of personal data in public clouds)
	•	ISO 27701 (privacy information management)
ISO certifications are issued by accredited certification bodies, although OpenAI's public pages do not identify the specific certifying organization. (OpenAI("https://openai.com/security-and-privacy/?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
Evidence classification:
	•	✅ Certification (vendor reports holding the certification)

Other relevant certifications and compliance programs
ISO 42001
OpenAI states it maintains ISO/IEC 42001:2023, an AI management system standard covering its consumer and business AI products. This is a relatively new international standard for AI management systems. (OpenAI("https://openai.com/security-and-privacy/?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
Evidence classification:
	•	Certification claimed by the vendor

HIPAA
OpenAI does not state that every product is HIPAA compliant.
Instead, it offers HIPAA-eligible products and will execute a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for specific offerings, including certain healthcare-focused ChatGPT products and API configurations. Organizations remain responsible for configuring and using those services appropriately to satisfy HIPAA requirements. (OpenAI Help Center("https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001069-chatgpt-healthcare-and-regulated-workspace-functionality?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
Evidence classification:
	•	⚠️ Vendor claim

PCI DSS
OpenAI states it maintains PCI DSS compliance for the ChatGPT components involved in delegated payment processing. The scope is limited to those payment-processing components rather than the entire platform. (OpenAI("https://openai.com/security-and-privacy/?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
Evidence classification:
	•	⚠️ Vendor claim

CSA STAR
OpenAI lists CSA STAR Level 1 for its API platform and ChatGPT business services.
CSA STAR Level 1 is based on a self-assessment published in the Cloud Security Alliance STAR registry; it is not equivalent to an independently audited STAR Level 2 certification. (OpenAI("https://openai.com/security-and-privacy/?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
Evidence classification:
	•	Vendor self-assessment / registry listing
