OpenAI company overview
Founding year and operating history
	•	Founded: December 2015.
	•	Headquarters: San Francisco, California, USA.
	•	Mission: To ensure artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits all of humanity.
	•	Time in operation: Approximately 10 years (2015–2026). (OpenAI("https://openai.com/our-structure/?stream=top&utm_source=chatgpt.com"))

Team size
The exact employee count is not published by OpenAI.
Recent reporting estimates:
	•	Approximately 4,500 employees (mid-2026). (The Information("https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/openai?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
Because OpenAI is private, this figure should be treated as an estimate rather than an official headcount.

Layoffs, leadership departures, or shutdown rumors
Layoffs
	•	No major company-wide layoffs have been publicly announced as of the latest available information.
	•	OpenAI has generally been expanding hiring rather than reducing headcount.
Leadership departures
OpenAI has experienced several high-profile executive and research departures over the past few years, including:
	•	Mira Murati (former CTO)
	•	Ilya Sutskever (co-founder and former Chief Scientist)
	•	Jan Leike (former safety leader)
	•	John Schulman (co-founder)
	•	Several senior safety and alignment researchers
More recently, OpenAI has continued reorganizing its research and safety organization, with additional leadership changes reported. (The Information("https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/openai?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
Shutdown rumors
	•	There are no credible reports indicating OpenAI is shutting down.
	•	Despite intense competition and high operating costs, the company continues to release new products, expand infrastructure, and raise significant capital. (OpenAI("https://openai.com/index/openai-launches-the-deployment-company/?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))

Financial distress or acquisition signs
Financial distress
There are no reliable indications that OpenAI is in imminent financial distress.
However:
	•	The company is widely reported to spend very large amounts on AI infrastructure, GPUs, research, and data centers.
	•	It has historically operated with substantial losses while pursuing rapid growth, which is common among frontier AI companies.
	•	The scale of its recent funding rounds suggests investors continue to support its long-term strategy. (OpenAI("https://openai.com/index/scaling-ai-for-everyone/?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
Acquisition
	•	No credible evidence suggests OpenAI is being acquired.
	•	Instead, OpenAI has made acquisitions itself (for example, to strengthen deployment capabilities) and continues to operate independently under its public benefit corporation structure controlled by the OpenAI Foundation. (OpenAI("https://openai.com/index/openai-launches-the-deployment-company/?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
