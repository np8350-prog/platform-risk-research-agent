OutSystems overview
	•	Founded: 2001 by Paulo Rosado in Lisbon, Portugal. The company has operated for approximately 25 years (2001–2026). (Wikipedia("https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OutSystems?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
	•	Funding rounds (publicly known):
	◦	2018: Raised $360M from KKR and Goldman Sachs (reported as a major growth investment). (Wikipedia("https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OutSystems?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
	◦	2021: Raised $150M at a reported $9.5B valuation, led by Abdiel Capital and Tiger Global. (Wikipedia("https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OutSystems?utm_source=chatgpt.com"))
	◦	2022: Reported investment from KKR of approximately €231.8M ($228M). Forbes reported this implied a valuation reduction from the prior $9.5B level to roughly $4.3B.
