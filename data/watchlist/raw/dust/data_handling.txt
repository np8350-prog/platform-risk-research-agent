Customer data is stored on Google Cloud Platform (in the USA or EU), with database provider Qdrant Solutions GmbH used for storing data embeddings (https://www.dust.tt/legal/security). Hosting region can be selected at workspace creation for Enterprise customers (https://www.dust.tt/legal/security). Dust and foundation model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, Google, Fireworks.ai) are prohibited from using Customer Data for model training (https://www.dust.tt/legal/security). Foundation Model Providers apply a "Zero Data Retention" policy, meaning Customer Content will not be logged for human review and will not be saved to disk or retained by those providers (https://www.dust.tt/legal/security). A full list of subprocessors and third parties is available in Dust's Sub-Processors Notice, including infrastructure providers (Google Cloud Platform, Qdrant, SendGrid, Datadog, E2B, Cloudflare), foundation model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, Google, Fireworks.ai, ElevenLabs), and operations providers (Customer.io, Front, Hubspot, Snowflake, Stripe, PostHog, Novu) (https://www.dust.tt/legal/subprocessors). No specific retention policy details were found beyond the zero retention policy for foundation model providers.
