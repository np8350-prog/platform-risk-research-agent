Amazon Bedrock is not a standalone AI SaaS company; it is a fully managed generative-AI service operated by Amazon Web Services (AWS). It was announced in April 2023 and became generally available on September 28, 2023. (https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/amazon-bedrock-is-now-generally-available-build-and-scale-generative-ai-applications-with-foundation-models/) (https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2023/09/amazon-bedrock-generally-available/)
	•	Founding year / operating history: Bedrock itself has operated commercially since September 2023; AWS is a long-established Amazon business rather than a venture-backed startup. There is no separate Bedrock founding date beyond its 2023 launch. (https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2023/09/amazon-bedrock-generally-available/)
	•	Funding: No separate funding rounds exist for Amazon Bedrock. It is an AWS service within Amazon, so startup-style funding rounds, investors, and valuation data are not applicable. No URL available for this specific negative finding.
	•	Team size: No reliable public headcount specifically for the Amazon Bedrock organization was found. AWS/Amazon does not publicly disclose a separate Bedrock employee count. No URL available for this.
	•	Layoffs: Amazon announced approximately 14,000 corporate-role reductions in October 2025 and approximately 16,000 additional role reductions in January 2026. These were Amazon-wide organizational reductions, not evidence that Bedrock itself was being shut down. (https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-workforce-reduction) (https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-layoffs-corporate-jan-2026)
	•	AI leadership changes: Amazon's AI organization underwent a significant restructuring in 2026. Rohit Prasad, who had led Amazon's AGI organization, departed in December 2025; David Luan left Amazon in February 2026; Amazon subsequently shut down its AGI Lab and reorganized its frontier-model effort. These changes concern Amazon's broader internal-model strategy rather than the Bedrock service itself. (https://www.businessinsider.com/amazon-overhauls-ai-strategy-phasing-out-most-nova-models-2026-7)
	•	Shutdown rumors: I found no credible evidence that Amazon intends to shut down Amazon Bedrock. In fact, Bedrock remains an active AWS service with continuing model, inference, agent, security and compliance development. (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/)
	•	Model/service continuity risk: There is a meaningful model-level lifecycle risk. AWS explicitly classifies models as Active, Legacy or End-of-Life. A model reaching EOL can stop accepting requests, requiring customers to migrate their application code; migration is not automatic. AWS currently states that models remain available for at least 12 months after launch before EOL, with a Legacy period before EOL. (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/model-lifecycle.html)
	•	Financial distress/acquisition: No credible evidence was found of AWS/Amazon being in financial distress, or of Bedrock being offered for acquisition. Bedrock is an internal AWS service, so startup acquisition risk is substantially lower than for an independent AI SaaS vendor. No URL available for the negative finding.
	•	Overall stability assessment: LOW vendor-failure risk / MEDIUM product-evolution risk. The underlying vendor is Amazon/AWS, but the rapidly changing foundation-model portfolio creates migration and model-availability risk.
