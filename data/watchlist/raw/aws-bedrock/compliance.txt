Amazon Bedrock is built on AWS's compliance foundation. AWS states that Bedrock is "in scope for common compliance standards":- **SOC 2**: Bedrock operates within AWS's SOC 2 certified infrastructure. SOC 2 Type II is referenced in multiple sources. *This is AWS's own claim, supported by third-party references; independent audit reports are available via AWS Artifact.*- **GDPR**: Bedrock is designed to support GDPR compliance. AWS states customers can use Bedrock in compliance with GDPR. *This is AWS's own claim.*- **ISO 27001**: AWS states Bedrock is in scope for ISO 27001. *Vendor claim.*- **HIPAA**: Bedrock is "HIPAA eligible", meaning it can be configured to handle protected health information under a Business Associate Agreement. *Vendor claim.*- **FedRAMP**: Bedrock is a "FedRAMP High authorized service". *Vendor claim.*- **PCI DSS**: Referenced as a compliance standard Bedrock supports. *Vendor claim.*- **CSA STAR Level 2**: Also mentioned as in scope. *Vendor claim.***Important caveat**: While the Bedrock *service* inherits AWS compliance certifications, when using third-party models (e.g., Anthropic's Claude) through Bedrock, customers should verify each model provider's own compliance posture, as data may flow to those providers depending on configuration.
