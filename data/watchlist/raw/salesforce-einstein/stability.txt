•	Founding & History: Salesforce, Inc. (NYSE: CRM) was founded in March 1999 and has operated for over 27 years. Salesforce Einstein was launched in September 2016 as a native AI suite, and expanded into the Einstein 1 Platform and Agentforce.  
	•	Funding: As a publicly traded Fortune 500 company (IPO in 2004), Salesforce is funded via public equity markets and enterprise cash flow rather than traditional venture capital. Its AI initiatives are backed by multi-billion-dollar R&D allocations and corporate investments via Salesforce Ventures. 
	•	Team Size: Total Salesforce headcount is approximately 83,334 employees. The dedicated AI, Data Cloud, and Agentforce product and engineering divisions consist of several thousand employees.  
	•	Layoffs & Leadership: Salesforce underwent a major ~10% headcount reduction (~8,000 employees) in early 2023. Subsequent surgical cuts occurred, including cutting ~4,000 customer support roles as AI automation expanded, alongside minor workforce reshuffles. Prominent leadership shifts include the departure of former Salesforce AI CEO Clara Shih.  
	•	Financial Distress / Acquisition: There are no signs of financial distress or risk of acquisition. Salesforce generates ~$38B+ in annual revenue with a market capitalization exceeding $250 billion, positioning it as an acquirer rather than a target.
