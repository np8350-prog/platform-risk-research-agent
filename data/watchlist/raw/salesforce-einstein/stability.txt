Salesforce Einstein is not a standalone company; it is an AI product/platform operated by Salesforce, whose current AI portfolio increasingly combines Einstein capabilities with Agentforce. Salesforce was founded in 1999 and G2 lists approximately 83,838 employees for Salesforce. (https://www.salesforce.com/) (https://www.salesforce.com/en-us/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/documents/platform/Salesforce-Platform-G2-Cloud_PaaS_Winter_2026_V1.pdf)
	•	Funding: Salesforce is a public company, so Einstein does not have independent venture funding rounds. Salesforce's fiscal 2026 revenue was $41.5 billion, up 10% year over year. (https://investor.salesforce.com/resources/investor-faqs/) No URL available for Einstein-specific funding because it is not a separately financed entity.
	•	Current financial position: Salesforce reported $41.5B FY2026 revenue and continues to invest heavily in AI. (https://investor.salesforce.com/resources/investor-faqs/) There is no evidence from the sources reviewed of insolvency or financial distress.
	•	Team size: Salesforce does not publish a separate Einstein headcount. G2's 2026 Salesforce Platform profile lists 83,838 employees for Salesforce overall. (https://www.salesforce.com/en-us/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/documents/platform/Salesforce-Platform-G2-Cloud_PaaS_Winter_2026_V1.pdf)
	•	Layoffs: Salesforce reportedly cut fewer than 1,000 employees in February 2026, including roles in marketing, product management, data analytics and the Agentforce AI product team. (https://www.reuters.com/business/world-at-work/salesforce-cuts-less-than-1000-jobs-business-insider-reports-2026-02-10/)
	•	Salesforce also made additional cuts in June 2026, with reporting indicating that employees working on Agentforce, MuleSoft and Marketing Cloud were affected. (https://www.reddit.com/r/Layoffs/comments/1u1b778/salesforce_lays_off_employees_in_a_new_round_of/) This is a Reddit repost of reporting/regulatory-notice information, so it should be treated as secondary evidence.
	•	Salesforce's CEO said in 2025 that approximately 4,000 customer-support positions had been eliminated, attributing the reduction partly to AI-driven efficiency and reduced support demand. (https://www.reddit.com/r/Futurology/comments/1naowlm/salesforce_ceo_confirms_4000_layoffs_because_i/)
	•	Leadership: Salesforce's engineering organization has been led by Srini Tallapragada, President and Chief Engineering and Customer Success Officer, who has been responsible for Salesforce's platform, applications, infrastructure and security and has led work including Einstein, Hyperforce and Data Cloud. (https://www.salesforce.com/news/srinivas_tallapragada/)
	•	Salesforce has experienced senior leadership turnover: Bret Taylor left the co-CEO role in January 2023, returning to entrepreneurship. (https://www.axios.com/2022/11/30/bret-taylor-salesforce-ceo-step-down) In 2026, Salesforce also made further executive changes, including promoting Miguel Milano to COO following the resignation of the Chief Engineering Officer. (https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/salesforce-promotes-miguel-milano-to-chief-operating-officer-under-ceo-marc-benioff/articleshow/132985240.cms)
	•	Acquisitions: Salesforce announced a definitive agreement in June 2026 to acquire Fin, formerly Intercom, for approximately $3.6 billion, demonstrating continued investment in AI/customer-agent technology rather than retrenchment from AI. (https://investor.salesforce.com/news/news-details/2026/Salesforce-Signs-Definitive-Agreement-to-Acquire-Fin/default.aspx)
	•	Product retirement: Salesforce announced in March 2026 that Einstein Prediction Builder is being retired and recommended migration to Data Cloud's Einstein Studio Model Builder. Existing subscriptions remain available until their end date, after which associated customer data will be deleted. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=004980243&language=en_US&type=1)
	•	Shutdown rumors: I found no credible evidence that Salesforce intends to shut down Einstein as a whole. Instead, individual Einstein products are being retired or repositioned as Salesforce consolidates its AI strategy around Data Cloud and Agentforce. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=004980243&language=en_US&type=1)
	•	Overall stability: LOW vendor-failure risk / MEDIUM product-lifecycle risk. Salesforce is financially and operationally very stable, but the retirement of Einstein Prediction Builder demonstrates that individual Einstein features should not be assumed to have indefinite continuity.
