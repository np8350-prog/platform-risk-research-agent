•	API: Salesforce exposes extensive APIs for interacting with CRM objects, metadata, data and platform services. Einstein/Agentforce functionality can be incorporated into Salesforce applications through the Salesforce platform and APIs. (https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/apis)
	•	SDK/development: Salesforce supports Apex, Lightning Web Components, Flow and multiple APIs/SDKs for building custom functionality on the platform. (https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform)
	•	No-code: Einstein integrates with Salesforce's Flow, Prompt Builder and other declarative configuration tools, allowing administrators to configure AI capabilities without writing traditional application code for many use cases. Salesforce explicitly includes Prompt Builder and Einstein for Flow within the Einstein Platform. (https://compliance.salesforce.com/en/services/agentforce-einstein-platform)
	•	Agentforce: Einstein/Agentforce can invoke actions against Salesforce data and business processes, with standard agent actions governed through the Salesforce platform and Trust Layer. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.copilot_data_usage.htm&language=en_US&type=5)
	•	External integrations: Salesforce has a large integration ecosystem through APIs, AppExchange and MuleSoft. Current Salesforce product material describes extensive connectivity through its platform and integration products. (https://www.salesforce.com/integration/)
	•	Data export: Salesforce provides APIs and data-export mechanisms, including Data Export Service and Bulk API, allowing customers to extract Salesforce records. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.admin_exportdata.htm&type=5)
	•	AI-specific portability: Exporting underlying CRM data is relatively straightforward compared with exporting Einstein-specific configuration, prompts, grounding rules, agent actions and Salesforce Flow logic. Those components are Salesforce-specific and may require redevelopment on another AI platform. Salesforce's developer and metadata architecture documents these platform-specific components. (https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform)
	•	Product retirement risk: Einstein Prediction Builder's retirement is an explicit example of feature-level migration risk. Salesforce recommends migration to Data Cloud's Einstein Studio Model Builder. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=004980243&language=en_US&type=1)
	•	Breaking/operational risk: Einstein Activity Capture has experienced multiple production incidents in 2026, including synchronization delays and backlogs. (https://status.salesforce.com/incidents/20003896) (https://status.salesforce.com/incidents/20004026)
	•	Dependency on Salesforce: Einstein is deeply dependent on Salesforce's underlying CRM/data model, Hyperforce infrastructure, identity/permissions model, Data Cloud where applicable, and Salesforce-specific APIs and metadata. (https://compliance.salesforce.com/en/services/agentforce-einstein-platform)
	•	Model-provider dependency: Agentforce can use third-party LLMs behind Salesforce's Trust Layer, creating an additional dependency on Salesforce's model-routing/provider ecosystem even though the customer-facing integration remains Salesforce-controlled. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.copilot_data_usage.htm&language=en_US&type=5)
	•	Lock-in: HIGH. Salesforce provides excellent portability for raw CRM data through APIs, but Einstein's greatest value comes from deep integration with Salesforce objects, permissions, workflows, Data Cloud, Prompt Builder, Flow and Agentforce. Recreating that architecture outside Salesforce would require substantial redevelopment.
	•	Community evidence of lock-in/complexity: Users continue to complain about Salesforce's complexity and the technical expertise required to extract and configure data, while Salesforce's current product direction is moving further toward integrated AI agents and Headless 360. (https://www.reddit.com/r/SalesforceAI/comments/1sfu7hh/7_salesforce_frustrations_that_ai_should_have/) (https://www.reddit.com/r/salesforce/comments/1spwfnv/is_anyone_else_feeling_headless/)
	•	Overall integration assessment: Very strong integration / HIGH lock-in. Einstein is most compelling when Salesforce is already the organization's system of record. For a company that is not deeply committed to Salesforce, adopting Einstein can create significant architectural dependency on Salesforce's CRM, Data Cloud, automation and AI ecosystem.
