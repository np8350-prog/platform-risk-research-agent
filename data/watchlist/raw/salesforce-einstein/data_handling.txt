•	Einstein data use varies by feature. Salesforce explicitly provides a feature-by-feature table identifying which customer data Einstein features use and which features use global models trained on aggregated customer data. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sales.sales_einstein_features_data_usage.htm&language=en_US&type=5)
	•	Global models: Some Einstein features can use global models trained on customer data to identify aggregated trends across Salesforce customers; Salesforce states that customers can opt out of the global model. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sales.sales_einstein_features_data_usage.htm&language=en_US&type=5)
	•	Einstein Conversation Insights / Activity Capture: Salesforce states that customer data from Einstein Conversation Insights and Einstein Activity Capture is not used to train global models. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sales.sales_einstein_features_data_usage.htm&language=en_US&type=5)
	•	Agentforce / Trust Layer: Salesforce states that Agentforce is integrated with the Einstein Trust Layer and that the Trust Layer uses zero-data retention, meaning data is not stored or used for model training by third-party LLMs. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.copilot_data_usage.htm&language=en_US&type=5)
	•	Salesforce also explicitly notes that some Einstein features can use global models, although those global models do not apply to the Agentforce platform and agent actions themselves; an agent can invoke other AI features that do use global models. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.copilot_data_usage.htm&language=en_US&type=5)
	•	Einstein Activity Capture data: Einstein Activity Capture can capture email, meeting and contact information from connected user mailboxes, process it through the Activity Platform on Hyperforce/AWS, and synchronize it with the Salesforce main instance. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=aac_data_retention.htm&language=en_US&type=5)
	•	Retention: Einstein Activity Capture data stored on the Activity Platform is retained for 6–24 months depending on the license. Standard users have a six-month retention period; organizations with qualifying paid Einstein licenses generally have a 24-month default retention period. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=aac_data_retention.htm&language=en_US&type=5)
	•	De-identified data: Salesforce states that it retains de-identified activity data for up to two years and may use it with machine-learning models and third-party data enrichment; the normal activity-retention policy does not apply to this de-identified data. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.aac_data_retention.htm&language=en_US&type=5)
	•	Data residency: Salesforce's Hyperforce architecture provides regional data-residency controls, but some Einstein features can process data outside the customer's geographical location. Salesforce states that the United States and Europe maintain local-region data residency, while Australia, Canada, India and Japan can have Einstein processing outside the local geography. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=mktg.mc_anb_data_residency_hyperforce.htm&language=en_US&type=5)
	•	Specific residency limitation: Salesforce states that Einstein Copy Insights' Emotional Tone Widget does not provide data residency and cannot be independently disabled from Einstein Copy Insights. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=mktg.mc_anb_data_residency_hyperforce.htm&language=en_US&type=5)
	•	Infrastructure: Salesforce documents the locations of Einstein Platform and Agentforce data centers and states that the information applies to Einstein Vision, Einstein Language, Einstein Prediction Builder, Einstein GPT, Agentforce, Prompt Builder and numerous other Einstein services. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=000396615&language=en_US&type=1)
	•	Third-party LLMs: Agentforce can use third-party large language models through Salesforce's Trust Layer; Salesforce states that the Trust Layer prevents third-party LLM providers from retaining or training on customer data under the zero-retention architecture. (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.copilot_data_usage.htm&language=en_US&type=5)
	•	Customer-data isolation: Salesforce's Einstein architecture uses the Trust Layer to ground AI requests in customer-authorized Salesforce data and apply security controls before sending data to an LLM. (https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/what-is-the-einstein-trust-layer/)
	•	Overall data-handling assessment: MEDIUM risk, potentially LOW with correct configuration. The Trust Layer and zero-retention architecture are strong controls, but Einstein is not one uniform data-processing service. Feature-specific global-model participation, activity capture, retention, regional processing and third-party model routing must be assessed individually.
