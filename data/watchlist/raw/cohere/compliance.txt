1. SOC 2 Status
	•	Status: SOC 2 Type II (covering the Trust Services Criteria of Security, Confidentiality, and Availability).  
	•	Vendor Claim vs. Independent Confirmation: 
	◦	Vendor Claim: Cohere lists SOC 2 Type II compliance on its website ([cohere.com/security](https://cohere.com/security)) and Trust Center (trustcenter.cohere.com). 
	◦	Independent Confirmation: Independently confirmed via third-party audit. SOC 2 Type II compliance is validated through an annual independent audit by a licensed CPA firm. The full audit report is available for download directly from Cohere's Trust Portal under a mutual NDA.  
2. ISO 27001 Status
	•	Status: ISO 27001 (Information Security Management System) certified.  
	•	Vendor Claim vs. Independent Confirmation: 
	◦	Vendor Claim: Published on Cohere’s Trust Center.  
	◦	Independent Confirmation: Independently confirmed. ISO certification requires formal audits and issuance of an official certificate by an accredited, independent third-party registrar. Cohere provides access to the official third-party ISO 27001 certificate in its document library.  
3. GDPR Compliance
	•	Status: GDPR compliant with a standardized multi-jurisdictional Data Processing Addendum (DPA), Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), and EU intra-region data routing options.  
	•	Vendor Claim vs. Independent Confirmation: 
	◦	Vendor Claim: Vendor self-claim / contractual representation. Cohere self-asserts GDPR compliance in its terms, privacy policies, and DPA.  
	◦	Independent Confirmation: Not independently certifiable. There is no single, universally recognized global "GDPR certification body." While Cohere uses compliance automation platforms (Secureframe) for internal GDPR training and offers contractual DPAs and Transfer Impact Assessments (TIAs), GDPR compliance remains a vendor-backed contractual commitment rather than an external accredited seal.
