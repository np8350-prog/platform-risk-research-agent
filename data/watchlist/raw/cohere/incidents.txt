3. Public Security Incidents & Vulnerability Disclosures
Terrarium Sandbox Remote Code Execution (CVE-2026-5752)
	•	Date: Disclosed April 2026. 
	•	Incident: Security researchers discovered a critical vulnerability (CVSS score 9.3) in Terrarium, Cohere's open-source Python sandbox used to execute untrusted code. The flaw stemmed from a JavaScript prototype chain traversal within the Pyodide WebAssembly runtime environment.  
	•	Impact: Attackers could break out of the execution sandbox without prior user privileges, allowing arbitrary system command execution with root privileges on host servers.  
	•	How Known: Public CVE records and cybersecurity news coverage (The Hacker News).  
4. Lawsuits Related to Data Handling, Copyright & Security
Publishers Class Action Copyright Lawsuit (Advance Local Media v. Cohere)
	•	Date: Filed February 2025 in U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York (SDNY); ongoing through 2026. 
	•	Allegations: A coalition of 14 major media publishers—including Condé Nast (The New Yorker, Vogue), The Atlantic, Forbes, and Vox Media—sued Cohere for systematic copyright and trademark infringement. The complaint alleges that Cohere unlawfully scraped over 4,000 copyrighted articles (allegedly bypassing paywalls and robots.txt scraper restrictions) to train its Command model family and power its real-time Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) search features without paying licensing fees.  
	•	Cohere’s Position: Cohere publicly rejected the lawsuit as "frivolous and misguided," asserting that its model training processes constitute transformative fair use and comply with intellectual property standards. 
	•	How Known: SDNY court filings and legal press (BakerHostetler Case Tracker, ComplexDiscovery).
