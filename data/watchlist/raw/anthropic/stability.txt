Overview
Anthropic (legally Anthropic, PBC) is an artificial intelligence research and public benefit corporation headquartered in San Francisco, California. It is best known for its frontier LLM assistant family, Claude.

1. Founding Year & Operational Duration
	•	Founding Date: January 26, 2021.  
	•	Founders: Founded by seven former OpenAI employees, led by siblings Dario Amodei (CEO) and Daniela Amodei (President) alongside Jared Kaplan, Jack Clark, Chris Olah, Ben Mann, and Sam McCandlish.  
	•	Operational Duration: Anthropic has been operating for ~5.5 years.  
2. Major Funding Rounds & Valuation
Anthropic is one of the most heavily capitalized private technology companies in history. Key funding milestones include:

	•	Series A (May 2021): $124 million led by Jaan Tallinn, with participation from Dustin Moskovitz and Eric Schmidt.  
	•	Series B (April 2022): $580 million led by Sam Bankman-Fried and FTX executives (these shares were later liquidated/repurchased through bankruptcy court proceedings).  
	•	Series C (May 2023): $450 million led by Spark Capital, with Google, Salesforce Ventures, and Zoom.  
	•	Amazon Strategic Partnership (2023–2024): Amazon initially committed $1.25 billion in September 2023, completed a second $2.75 billion tranche in March 2024, and added an additional $4 billion in late 2024, bringing Amazon's total strategic commitment to $8 billion.  
	•	Google Strategic Investment (2023–2024): Google invested over $2 billion across convertible notes and direct equity.  
	•	Series E & Series F (2025): Raised multiple tranches including a $13 billion Series F in late 2025 led by ICONIQ and Lightspeed Venture Partners at a $183 billion post-money valuation.  
	•	Series G (February 2026): $30 billion round led by Coatue, GIC, MGX, Founders Fund, Microsoft, Nvidia, and Sequoia Capital at a post-money valuation of $380 billion.  
3. Team Size & Growth
	•	Current Team Size: Estimated between 3,000 and 3,800+ employees globally.  
	•	Historical Growth: 
	◦	2021: ~7 employees  
	◦	2023: ~240–500 employees  
	◦	2024: ~1,000+ employees  
	◦	2025–2026: ~3,000–3,800+ employees  
	•	Distribution: ~73% of workforce is based in the United States (primarily San Francisco), with expanding operations in the UK, India, Ireland, and Japan.  
4. Layoffs, Leadership Departures & Shutdown Rumors
	•	Layoffs: None reported. The company has been aggressively hiring rather than downsizing, expanding its workforce by over 600% between 2023 and 2026.  
	•	Leadership Changes: 
	◦	Dario Amodei (CEO) and Daniela Amodei (President) remain at the helm of the company.  
	◦	Mike Krieger (former Instagram co-founder who joined as Chief Product Officer in 2024) transitioned to lead a specialized research division called "Labs".  
	◦	Co-founder Jack Clark launched the Anthropic Institute think tank to study long-term AI governance.  
	◦	The company continues to recruit high-profile researchers from competitors (e.g., Nobel laureate John Jumper joined Anthropic from Google DeepMind).  
	•	Shutdown Rumors: None. 
5. Signs of Financial Distress or Acquisition
	•	Financial Distress: None. The company possesses tens of billions in cash reserves, institutional debt/credit facilities, and backings from major tech hyperscalers. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) has grown significantly into the multi-billion-dollar range.  
	•	Acquisition Status: Anthropic is not being acquired. It operates as an independent Delaware Public Benefit Corporation (PBC) governed by a Long-Term Benefit Trust to safeguard its safety mandate. Amazon and Google hold non-controlling minority stakes.  
	•	IPO Outlook: Rather than an acquisition target, financial reports indicate Anthropic submitted a confidential draft S-1 filing to the SEC to explore an Initial Public Offering (IPO).
