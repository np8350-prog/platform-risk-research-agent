Anthropic was founded in January 2021 by former OpenAI executives and researchers, including siblings Dario Amodei (CEO) and Daniela Amodei (President), alongside Jared Kaplan, Jack Clark, Sam McCandlish, Tom Brown, and Benjamin Mann (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic).

The company has executed multiple massive venture and strategic funding rounds:

	•	May 2021: Raised $124 million in Series A funding led by Jaan Tallinn (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic). 
	•	April 2022: Raised $580 million in Series B funding led by Sam Bankman-Fried and participating venture investors (https://startupintros.com/orgs/anthropic). 
	•	March 2023: Secured $300 million in strategic funding from Google (https://startupintros.com/orgs/anthropic). 
	•	May 2023: Raised $450 million in Series C funding led by Spark Capital (https://startupintros.com/orgs/anthropic). 
	•	August 2023: Secured $100 million in Series C Plus expansion funding (https://startupintros.com/orgs/anthropic). 
	•	September 2023 – March 2024: Amazon committed up to $4 billion in strategic funding and cloud partnership investments (https://startupintros.com/orgs/anthropic). 
	•	October 2023 – January 2025: Google expanded its investment commitments with multi-billion-dollar financing tranches (https://startupintros.com/orgs/anthropic). 
	•	February 2024: Raised $880 million in Series E funding (https://startupintros.com/orgs/anthropic). 
	•	March 2025: Raised $3.5 billion in funding led by Lightspeed Venture Partners (https://startupintros.com/orgs/anthropic). 
	•	September 2025: Raised $13 billion in Series F funding led by Fidelity and Lightspeed (https://startupintros.com/orgs/anthropic). 
	•	February 2026: Raised a $30 billion Series G round led by Coatue, D. E. Shaw, Founders Fund, ICONIQ Capital, and MGX (https://startupintros.com/orgs/anthropic). 
In total, Anthropic has raised over $74 billion across its lifetime with private valuations reaching between $18 billion and $900+ billion in private secondary and financing estimates (https://startupintros.com/orgs/anthropic) (https://tracxn.com/d/companies/anthropic/__SzoxXDMin-NK5tKB7ks8yHr6S9Mz68pjVCzFEcGFZ08). The workforce is estimated at approximately 1,000 to 2,500 employees (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic) (https://startupintros.com/orgs/anthropic).
