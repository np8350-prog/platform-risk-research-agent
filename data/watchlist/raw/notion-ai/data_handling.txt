•	Data Collected & Stored: Customer content (text, blocks, database entries, uploaded files), AI prompt queries, generated AI outputs, session telemetry, and account metadata (https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-security-practices) (https://trust.notion.com/). 
	•	Hosting Location: All customer data and AI vector embeddings are hosted on cloud infrastructure in Amazon Web Services (AWS) data centers (https://trust.notion.com/). 
	•	Data Retention Policy: Active account data is retained continuously. When a page or workspace is deleted, content can be restored for 30 days, after which it is permanently purged within 60 days (https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-security-practices). For AI features: Enterprise plan workspaces operate under zero data retention by LLM providers during processing; non-Enterprise workspaces retain AI prompt data with LLM providers for up to 30 days before deletion (https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-security-practices). Vector embeddings are purged within 60 days of workspace deletion (https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-security-practices). 
	•	Model Training Policy: Customer data is strictly never used to train or fine-tune Notion AI models or third-party partner LLMs; contractual agreements with LLM vendors explicitly enforce this restriction (https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-security-practices) (https://trust.notion.com/). 
	•	Subprocessors & Third Parties: Primary AI subprocessors include OpenAI (used for zero-retention embeddings and LLM inference) and Anthropic (Claude models), alongside cloud infrastructure providers AWS and Turbopuffer (vector storage) (https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-security-practices). Subprocessor changes are published with advance email notification available via team@makenotion.com (https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-security-practices).
