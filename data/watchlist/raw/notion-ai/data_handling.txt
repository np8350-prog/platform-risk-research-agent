Data Collection and Hosting
	•	Scope: Collects all pages, databases, and workspace content. Notion holds encryption keys (no default end-to-end encryption). 
	•	Infrastructure: Data hosted on AWS, Cloudflare, and Google Cloud.   Notion Mail specifically utilized AWS us-west-2. 
	•	Subprocessors: Data is shared with AWS, Anthropic, OpenAI, Runlayer, Stripe, and Cloudflare.
