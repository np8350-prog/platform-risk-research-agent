Notion Labs, Inc. was founded in 2013 by Ivan Zhao and Simon Last (https://research.contrary.com/report/notion).

The company's disclosed funding history includes:

	•	Seed / Early Rounds: Raised $2 million in angel funding in May 2015 and a $3.2 million Seed Plus round in June 2016 (https://startupintros.com/orgs/notion). 
	•	Series A: Raised $10 million in July 2019 at an $800 million valuation led by First Round Capital and angel investors (https://startupintros.com/orgs/notion). 
	•	Series B: Raised $50 million on April 2, 2020, at a $2 billion valuation led by Index Ventures (https://startupintros.com/orgs/notion). 
	•	Series C: Raised $275 million on October 8, 2021, at a $10 billion valuation led by Coatue Management and Sequoia Capital (https://startupintros.com/orgs/notion). Total funding raised across all rounds stands at approximately $340 million to $418 million (https://startupintros.com/orgs/notion). 
Notion grew from a lean team of under 50 employees during its early unicorn phase to over 500+ employees globally (https://medium.com/@sherrysun/notion-from-near-collapse-to-a-10b-all-in-one-workspace-unicorn-6113f7e1dc4c). During a major product rebuild in 2015, the co-founders laid off their initial 4-person team before relaunching Notion 2.0 (https://research.contrary.com/report/notion); however, no widespread company layoffs have occurred since achieving unicorn status ("no URL available for this"). Executive leadership has remained stable under co-founders Ivan Zhao and Simon Last, alongside early COO Akshay Kothari, with no high-profile executive departures reported ("no URL available for this"). There are no active shutdown rumors, insolvency signals, or acquisition threats ("no URL available for this"). Notion acted as an acquirer by buying privacy-focused email startup Skiff in February 2024 to launch Notion Mail (https://research.contrary.com/report/notion).
