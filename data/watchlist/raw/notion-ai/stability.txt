Notion Labs, Inc. is a mature entity founded in 2013, operating for approximately 13 years as of August 2026, with strong financial backing but conflicting employee count data. 

Founding and Funding History
Notion was incorporated on March 8, 2013, in San Francisco by Ivan Zhao, Simon Last, and three other co-founders. 

 The company has raised approximately $418M across 9 funding rounds. 

	•	Series D (2021): Raised $275M led by Coatue and Sequoia Capital, achieving a $10B post-money valuation. 
	•	Series C (2020): Raised $50M led by Index Ventures at a $2B valuation. 
	•	Current Valuation: Estimates range between $10B and $11B as of late 2025/2026. 
	•	Investors: Key backers include Accel, First Round Capital, and GIC. 
Team Size and Workforce
Employee counts vary significantly across sources, reflecting rapid growth or differing counting methodologies:
	•	March 2026: Reported 2,969 employees (54.7% growth from 2023). 
	•	Mid-2026 Estimates: Range from 4,800 to 7,321 employees. 
	•	Hiring Status: The company maintains 148 open roles as of April 2026, indicating active expansion. 
	•	Culture: Glassdoor rating stands at 4.3/5.0, with high marks for work-life balance. 
Layoffs and Product Shutdowns
	•	Layoffs: Anecdotal reports on employee forums (e.g., TeamBlind) mention layoffs in 2026, with some employees citing visa complications. No official mass layoff announcement was found in major news outlets. 
	•	Product Shutdown: Notion Mail is scheduled to shut down on September 22, 2026.   This decision follows a strategic shift toward "agentic AI" for inboxes, noting that over 50% of users managed emails without opening the inbox. This is a product consolidation, not a sign of company-wide financial distress.
