•	Integration Methods: Integrates via a public REST API, official client SDKs (e.g., JavaScript @notionhq/client), webhooks, and pre-built connectors for platforms like Slack, Jira, GitHub, Asana, Zapier, Nightfall DLP, and Datadog SIEM (https://www.notion.com/help/add-security-and-compliance-integrations). 
	•	Data Export & Exit Ease: Workspaces can be exported on demand in HTML, Markdown, or CSV formats (for databases) with attached file assets packaged in a ZIP archive (https://www.notion.com/help/export-your-content). Individual pages can also be exported to PDF (https://www.notion.com/help/export-your-content). 
	•	Export Policy Changes: Notion is phasing out full workspace-level PDF exports between now and August 31, 2026, while retaining full workspace HTML, Markdown, and CSV export capabilities (https://www.notion.com/help/back-up-your-data). 
	•	Integration Limits & Lock-in Risk: The Notion REST API enforces a rate limit of 3 requests per second per integration token, which can restrict large real-time data syncs (https://hackceleration.com/labs/review/notion). Vendor lock-in risk is moderate-to-high: while raw text exports cleanly as Markdown, complex relational database logic, rollups, custom formulas, and proprietary block layouts do not map 1:1 to other platforms upon export (https://hackceleration.com/labs/review/notion) (https://checkthat.ai/brands/notion-labs-inc/reviews).
