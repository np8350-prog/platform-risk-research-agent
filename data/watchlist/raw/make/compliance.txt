•	SOC 2 Type II: Make states that it is SOC 2 Type II compliant/certified. Make's security page states that its information-security program operates within infrastructure compliant with SOC 2 Type II, and its current product pages explicitly advertise SOC 2 Type II. (https://www.make.com/en/security) (https://www.make.com/en)
	•	Independent audit: Make announced in its community in June 2025 that it had successfully completed a SOC 2 Type II audit. (https://community.make.com/t/information-security-soc2/58171) The public source reviewed does not name the SOC 2 auditor. No URL available for a named auditor.
	•	Important timeline: In October 2024, Make publicly acknowledged that it was not yet SOC 2 certified and was working toward certification; in June 2025 it announced completion of the Type II audit. (https://community.make.com/t/information-security-soc2/58171) This indicates the current Type II status is materially newer than some older vendor-risk questionnaires may suggest.
	•	ISO 27001: Make states that its Enterprise platform operates under an information-security program that is ISO 27001 certified. (https://www.make.com/en/security) Make's terms also explicitly link to Celonis SE ISO/IEC 27001:2022 documentation. (https://www.make.com/en/terms-and-conditions)
	•	ISO registrar/auditor: The public Make pages reviewed do not identify the independent ISO registrar/certification body. No URL available for a named registrar.
	•	GDPR: Make states that it supports GDPR requirements, including purpose limitation, storage limitation, deletion rights and appropriate mechanisms for international data transfers. (https://www.make.com/en/privacy-and-gdpr) Make's terms also provide a DPA and EU-specific standard contractual clauses. (https://www.make.com/en/terms-and-conditions)
	•	Nature of GDPR claim: GDPR is not a certification. Make's GDPR statements are vendor claims describing its privacy program and contractual mechanisms rather than an independent GDPR certificate. (https://www.make.com/en/privacy-and-gdpr)
	•	HIPAA / BAA: Make does not appear to offer HIPAA compliance/BAA based on the current public evidence reviewed. In February 2023, a Make representative explicitly said HIPAA was not in place. A March 2025 Make community response repeated that Make was not HIPAA compliant. (https://community.make.com/t/is-make-hipaa-compliant/7702/2) (https://community.make.com/t/is-make-com-hipaa-compliant/71880)
	•	PCI DSS: Make's security documentation discusses PCI DSS as a security standard and encryption requirement, but I found no reliable evidence that Make itself holds a PCI DSS Attestation of Compliance. (https://help.make.com/methods-of-securing-data) No URL available for a Make PCI DSS certification.
	•	FedRAMP: I found no reliable evidence that Make is FedRAMP authorized. No URL available for this.
	•	Overall compliance: SOC 2 Type II: confirmed by vendor-announced independent audit; ISO 27001: vendor-confirmed through Celonis/Make certification documentation; GDPR: vendor compliance claim/contractual framework; HIPAA/BAA: not supported based on public evidence; PCI DSS/FedRAMP: not verified.
