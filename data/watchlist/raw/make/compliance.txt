Make publicly claims the following certifications on its website and security documentation:- **SOC 2 Type II:** Make states it has **"completed SOC 2 Type II and SOC 3 audits"**. The SOC 2 report details internal controls, while the SOC 3 report is public. *This is the vendor's own claim; the SOC 3 report is publicly accessible via Celonis's trust center.*- **ISO 27001:** Make states it **"operate[s] under an information security program that is ISO 27001 certified"**. *This is the vendor's own claim.*- GDPR: Make explicitly claims GDPR adherence and provides a **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** under Article 28 GDPR for paid plan customers starting from the Team tier. *This is the vendor's own claim.*- **Other:** Make offers **data encryption** (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.2/1.3 in transit), **SSO**, role-based access control, and audit logging. Enterprise customers receive **99.5% Cloud Service Uptime** SLA.**Note:** A community post from October 2024 indicated Make was "one of few that currently don't have SOC2", but the company's current security page clearly states SOC 2 Type II completion, suggesting this has since been obtained.
