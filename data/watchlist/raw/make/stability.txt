Make (formerly Integromat) is a no-code/low-code visual automation and integration platform operated by Celonis. Make describes itself as an automation and AI platform with 3,000+ app integrations and AI-agent capabilities. (https://www.make.com/en)
	•	Founding: Integromat was launched in 2012 in Prague. (https://techcrunch.com/2020/10/14/celonis-acquires-czech-startup-integromat-to-accelerate-move-to-process-automation/)
	•	Funding: Integromat had not raised outside funding before its acquisition, according to PitchBook information reported by TechCrunch. (https://techcrunch.com/2020/10/14/celonis-acquires-czech-startup-integromat-to-accelerate-move-to-process-automation/)
	•	Acquisition: Celonis acquired Integromat in October 2020. The exact transaction price was not disclosed, but Celonis CEO Alexander Rinke said it was more than $100 million. (https://techcrunch.com/2020/10/14/celonis-acquires-czech-startup-integromat-to-accelerate-move-to-process-automation/)
	•	Pre-acquisition scale: At the time of acquisition, Integromat had approximately 60 employees, more than 11,000 customers and a $10 million business, according to Celonis/TechCrunch reporting. (https://techcrunch.com/2020/10/14/celonis-acquires-czech-startup-integromat-to-accelerate-move-to-process-automation/)
	•	Current team size: Make currently states that it has 350+ Makers, representing more than 50 nationalities. (https://www.make.com/en/careers)
	•	Parent-company stability: Celonis was founded in 2011, became a unicorn in 2018, raised an additional $1 billion in 2021, and reached decacorn status; Celonis states that its 2022 Series D extension was $13 billion in valuation. (https://www.celonis.com/company/about-us?active-tab=tab-0)
	•	Layoffs: I found no reliable public evidence of a material Make-specific layoff or workforce reduction. No URL available for this negative finding.
	•	Leadership departures: I found no reliable public evidence of a founder/leadership departure that threatens Make's continuity. Make continues to recruit and describes itself as actively growing. (https://www.make.com/en/careers)
	•	Shutdown rumors: I found no credible evidence that Make is being shut down. The original Integromat product was intentionally retired as part of the migration to Make, rather than because of company failure. A 2023 community account reported that Integromat would be fully deactivated in September 2023. (https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/comments/16h27ho)
	•	Financial distress: I found no credible evidence of financial distress at Make or its parent Celonis. No URL available for this negative finding.
	•	Important product-continuity risk: The Integromat-to-Make transition demonstrates that Celonis is willing to retire an existing product architecture and require customers to migrate. A user who had built 150+ scenarios on Integromat reported that the transition involved substantial changes and dissatisfaction with the migration. This is a user report, not an independently verified statement by Make. (https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/comments/16h27ho)
	•	Overall stability: LOW vendor-failure risk / MEDIUM product-transition risk. Make benefits from being owned by Celonis, a large enterprise-software company, but the Integromat retirement is a concrete reminder that product/platform continuity should not be assumed indefinitely.
