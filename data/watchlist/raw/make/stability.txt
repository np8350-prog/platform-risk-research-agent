Make is the rebranded version of **Integromat**, a visual automation platform founded in **2012** (some sources cite 2015). The company is headquartered in **Prague, Czech Republic**.**Funding & Acquisition:** Integromat was a bootstrapped success story before being **acquired by German process mining leader Celonis in October 2020** for a sum reportedly **over $100 million**. Since the acquisition, Make's financial trajectory has been tied directly to Celonis—a late-stage powerhouse that has raised **over $1.7 billion** from top-tier investors including Accel, T. Rowe Price, and the Qatar Investment Authority, with its most recent round being a **$400 million Series D extension in August 2022** valuing the company at nearly **$13 billion**. Make itself is reported to have raised **$100M** in funding prior to acquisition.**Team size:** Make reports a **global team of 350+** employees, though third-party sources list approximately **709 people on record**.**Layoffs & leadership:** No public records of layoffs or major leadership departures at Make.**Financial distress / acquisition:** No signs of financial distress. Make is wholly owned by Celonis and viewed as a **critical component** of its parent company's strategy for turning process analytics into action. Analysts describe Make as a **"highly stable platform"** with longevity secured by Celonis's deep enterprise penetration and resources.
