**What data is collected and stored:** By default, **log data is stored for 30 days**. Enterprise plans offer extended storage. All passwords are stored in encrypted format and **"can't be reproduced by anyone—not even Make employees"**.**Where data is hosted:** Make's infrastructure resides within **Amazon AWS EC2 private instances (Amazon VPC)** deployed over **two zones** for availability. EU-based data processing is available via **AWS EU regions**.**Data retention policy:** Default log retention is **30 days**; extended retention available on Enterprise plans.**Model training:** Make does **not** claim to use customer data to train models. The platform acts as an orchestration layer, processing data through customer-configured scenarios.**Subprocessors/third parties:** Make shares data with **third-party service providers** as part of its automation workflows (the specific apps and services users connect to). The platform integrates with **over 3,000 apps**. Make also uses **AWS** as its cloud infrastructure provider.**Policy clarity:** The security and compliance policies are clearly documented on Make's website. However, specific details about all subprocessors and exact data flows for each integration are not exhaustively published—users should review the DPA and privacy policy for full details.
