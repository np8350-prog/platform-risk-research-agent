Appsmith was founded in **mid-2019** by Abhishek Nayak, Arpit Mohan, and Nikhil Nandagopal. It is a privately held, venture-backed company headquartered in San Francisco.**Funding:** Appsmith has raised a total of **$51.5M** across three rounds:- **Seed (August 2019):** $2.5M led by Accel- **Series A (October 2021):** $8M led by Canaan Partners- **Series B (June 2022):** $41M ($42M per PitchBook) led by Insight Partners, with participation from Accel, Canaan Partners, OSS Capital, and angel investors**Team size:** Employee counts vary across sources—Crustdata reports ~48 employees as of June 2026, PitchBook reports 94, and Inc42 reported 95 in 2022. Crustdata shows headcount fell 36% year-over-year.**Layoffs:** In **September 2023**, Appsmith laid off **35 employees (~25% of its workforce)**. CEO Abhishek Nayak attributed this to "challenging market conditions," a slowdown in business, and a shift toward "operational efficiency" and "sustainable growth". Impacted employees received a two-month severance package.**Leadership departures:** A key person exit—Pranav Kanade (Director of AI Solutions)—has been noted. No other major leadership departures or shutdown rumors are documented.**Financial distress / acquisition:** No public signs of financial distress or acquisition rumors. Appsmith remains an independent, private company. The company continues to release new products (e.g., Appsmith Agents in May 2025) and maintains an active open-source community.
